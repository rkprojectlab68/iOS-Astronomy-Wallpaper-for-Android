# iOS-Astronomy-Wallpaper-for-Android by RK
(reyxx_fgg)
iOS Astronomy-Inspired Interactive Live Wallpaper for Android is an interactive 3D engine-based live wallpaper application that brings detailed 3D models of all planets in the Solar System to your screen, complete with star animations, cinematic lighting, and elegant orbit lines.

Interaction & Animation Specs

Lock Screen: The selected planet stays centered. Tapping the planet reveals the Solar System, allowing you to select other planets. Tapping a different planet smoothly transitions it to the center.

Unlock: The planet smoothly glides and moves down off-screen.

Return to Lock Screen: When the screen turns on while locked, an animation brings the planet sliding back up from the bottom to the center.

iOS-Style Animation: The camera flies towards the planet upon tapping it. The orbits, Sun, and other planets expand and rotate with a smooth roll effect reminiscent of iOS. Background stars move across 3 parallax layers, while the Sun and other planets smoothly fade out as the camera zooms in.


How to Use:
• ​Open the app.

• ​Tap "Enable Lock Screen Tap(aktifkan tap di lockscreen)".

• ​Enable Accessibility service. This permission allows the wallpaper to respond to taps on the lock screen; without it, interactive taps will not work.

• ​Tap "Apply Wallpaper(pasang wallpaper)" and set it.

​Note: This application is still under development, so performance may not be fully optimized. Further updates and fixes will be rolled out.
